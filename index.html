A GENTLE REMINDER

Small steps count, too.

Looking after yourself doesn't have to be complicated. Start with small, manageable habits.

  • ♡ Take breaks when you need them.
  • ♡ Talk to someone you trust.
  • ♡ Make time for things you enjoy.
  • ♡ Try a few slow, comfortable breaths.
  • ♡ Ask for help when things feel difficult.
